Privacy policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · This is a static website. We designed it to respect your privacy by default.

1. What we collect

Nothing directly. This site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising and no fingerprinting. We do not collect, store or sell personal data.

2. Local storage (on your device only)

We save one preference — your chosen color theme — in your browser’s localStorage under the key ubh-theme. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

3. Third-party services

To display the site, your browser requests resources from these providers. They may receive standard connection data (such as your IP address) and process it under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • jsDelivr — CDN referenced in the documentation for serving the font (jsDelivr privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts — serves the UI typefaces used by this site’s interface (Google privacy policy). The Umar Butt Handwriting font itself is served from this site, not Google.
  • GitHub Sponsors embeds — the Support page embeds iframes from github.com; GitHub’s policy applies inside those frames.
  • Image hosts — the profile photo is served from ibb.co and the social preview image from raw.githubusercontent.com.

4. Using the font on your own website

If you self-host the font files, your visitors make no third-party requests for the font — the most privacy-friendly option, recommended for GDPR-sensitive projects. If you hotlink via jsDelivr, visitors’ browsers contact jsDelivr’s CDN; consult their policy above. Either way, the OFL license imposes no tracking or reporting obligations on you.

5. Children

This site is a general-audience font showcase and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or reach out via LinkedIn or Instagram.
