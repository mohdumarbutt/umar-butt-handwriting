About the font

A personal hand, digitized.

Umar Butt Handwriting turns real, everyday handwriting into a clean digital typeface — warm, casual and unmistakably human.

SPECIMEN — REGULAR 400

Every letter carries a little of the hand that wrote it.

The story

Umar Butt Handwriting is the personal handwriting of Mohd Umar Butt, digitized and refined into an everyday typeface. The goal was simple: capture the natural rhythm of real handwriting — its casual flow, its little imperfections — while keeping it clean enough to render beautifully on screens of every size.

Version 1.0.0 ships in a single Regular (400) style, packaged as a compact WOFF2 for the web and a TTF for desktop apps. It is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which means it is free forever — for personal projects, client work and commercial products alike.

Where it shines

Signatures & watermarks Quotes & social posts Greeting cards Hero headings Brand accents Education material App onboarding

Design tip: like all handwriting faces, it works best at display sizes and for short bursts of text. For long body paragraphs, pair it with a readable sans-serif or serif.
